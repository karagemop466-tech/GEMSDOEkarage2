### 1b. Why GEMSDOE32 h33-h33-2-b2 scored 0.2778, and whether higher is possible

**FILE-VERIFIED:** the 0.2778 file's dot set is a subset of the D2.8 dot set. The D2.8 reference has 44,090 dots; 6,436 were removed, leaving 37,654. Nothing was added. Audit section 5 of `evidence/gw-nfs/audit.json` records this. The score itself stays **OWNER-REPORTED**; no organizer receipt was found.

**Mechanism (first-order, not proven):** under alpha + beta = 1, a dot helps only when its hit probability exceeds about alpha × DTI. At DTI 0.2778 that threshold is about 0.2 × 0.2778 ≈ 0.056. If the owner's 0.2778 is higher than the D2.8 file's own score (that score is not in this repo and is not verified), removing 6,436 dots is consistent with those dots having had hit rates below that threshold. We cannot confirm this from the file alone: the removed dots were not scored on any holdout here.

**Is something higher possible?** In principle, yes. The rules score against a hidden test set, and the metric does reward correct dots. Nothing in this repository shows a verified method that beats 0.2778. The fixed-budget evidence we do have points the other way (section 1c). Realistic routes are the untested hypotheses in section 4, each to be validated first on a spatially blocked holdout.
