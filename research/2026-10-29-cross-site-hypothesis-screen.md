# Cross-site hypothesis screen — 2026-10-29

**Status: proposal only. No experiment, raster, candidate, or submission slot was produced or authorized.** The review screened commit-pinned public reports and selected run-card/code-path inventories for all nine focus repositories. This is a novelty screen over reviewed source material, not proof that no related idea exists anywhere else.

## Prior-art exclusions after the fresh nine-site review

Do not label the prior 2026-10-28 gravity/topography, conductivity, strain, or seismicity slate as fleet-wide untried. Current reports and file inventories show extensive related work: gravity/topography and magnetic/radiometric fusion (GEMSDOE24/30/31/33); conductivity, strain, seismicity and multiple point-temperature/geochemistry recipes (GEMSDOE25/27/30/33/36); and DEM, channel-profile, lidar-scarp and geomorphology features (GEMSDOE27/30/33/36). In particular, GEMSDOE27 has `src/gems27/hydrochemistry.py` and channel-analysis code; GEMSDOE30 has `src/gemsdoe30/geothermal_chemistry.py` and lidar-morphology code. A multi-analyte chemistry idea and a channel-offset idea may still have unrun *specific formulations*, but neither is treated here as a cleanly new signal family.

The candidate exact tests below are unrun in the reviewed current-head evidence. Two are already proposals in sibling repositories; that is not the same as a tested result, and this document does not claim original or fleet-wide scientific novelty.

## Gate and stop decision

The top optical hypothesis cannot be tested in this checkout: `data/` and `.cache/` are absent, authenticated competition features/template are unavailable, there is no unambiguous assigned single-method paragraph, and the complete registry is not established. The three external sources are official and their product/listing or service pages were checked, but *exact study-area coverage and local dataset bytes* remain unverified. These facts do not authorize a feature experiment. If prerequisites later become valid, preregister only the assigned hypothesis and validate it on the shared, buffered segment holdout before any slot consideration; use the common evaluator and writer, feature-alone leakage canaries, the pre-placement surface gate, and the final-dot gate. Stop on any registry rule failure. Slot selection remains a separate action.

No score, projected score, candidate TIFF, or organizer acceptance is claimed here. Any later DTI must be labelled **HOLDOUT-DTI** with evaluator version, withheld positives and 95% CI. A competition result is **ORGANIZER-CONFIRMED** only with a submission-page receipt bound to the exact artifact.
