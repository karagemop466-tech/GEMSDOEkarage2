## Executive answer

The available byte audits say the GEMSDOE32 prediction is a subset of the larger D2.8 dot set, with 6,436 dots removed, and a subset of GEMSDOE31, with 2,545 dots removed. That makes a plausible *conditional* explanation for a reported increase: under the competition's asymmetric DTI (false-positive weight α=0.2; false-negative weight β=0.8), deleting weakly supported predictions can improve the precision/recall trade-off if the removed pixels contributed more false-positive penalty than true-positive credit. This does **not** establish why the reported value changed: the 0.2778 claim is not linked to the raster hash by an organizer receipt, the hidden labels are unavailable, and no controlled holdout in this review tested the deletion. No new-file score estimate is supportable.

The official problem page says the task is fault prediction, hidden test labels include expert-mapped faults absent from the public USGS database, and the scoring kernel has 300 m support. It specifies a single-layer float32 GeoTIFF, EPSG:32611, 100 m, bounds/grid matching training data, values in [0,1], and null/NaN outside bounds. These are official format/scoring statements, **not proof that a locally saved research TIFF will pass the portal**. See the [official problem and format page](https://www.drivendata.org/competitions/306/competition-doe-gems/page/967/).

### Candidate selection and mandatory pre-slot test

**No candidate is selected or promoted in this review.** The heat-flow idea is ranked first for new independent physical information, not for a promised leaderboard gain. Before it can be called viable, obtain and hash the USGS archive, verify coverage/CRS/units/quality and source lineage, and demonstrate that its residual field is usable on the competition grid. Then use the shared cached feature stack, `scripts/evaluate_holdout.py`, and `scripts/submission_writer.py` only after the organizer training rasters/template are restored and an explicit method paragraph is assigned. Follow whole-segment buffered hide-and-recover, visible-only catalogue features, pixel-exact visible-fault masking, pooled DTI (α=0.2, β=0.8, 300 m triangular kernel), per-feature leakage canaries (AUC >0.90 is a stop), and a 95% spatial interval. No weekly submission slot is touched before the selector sees a positive holdout result and all uniqueness/format gates pass.
