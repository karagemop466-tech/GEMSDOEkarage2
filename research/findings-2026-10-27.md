## Why the user-reported GEMSDOE32 value might be high

The user's historical claim for `h33-h33-2-b2-20261004T220000Z-e5eb6e7e-zeros`
is **0.2778 USER-REPORTED / UNVERIFIED**, not an organizer-confirmed receipt.
The [nine pinned input bytes](../registry/focus_sites.json) were independently
read against an owner-mirrored grid. [The reproducible audit](../evidence/geometry-audit.json)
shows:

* GEMSDOE25 and GEMSDOE30 have identical positive masks within that footprint.
* The GEMSDOE32 positive mask is a strict subset of the historical GEMSDOE31
  mask: 2,545 points were removed, and no points were added. All 2,545 removed
  points lie more than 1 and at most 2 pixels from the mirrored known-fault
  mask; no GEMSDOE32 points remain within 2 pixels of that mask. This is a
  reproduced distance fact on the unauthenticated mirror, not hidden truth.
* The historical GEMSDOE36 mask contains that GEMSDOE32 mask plus 1,200 points.
* Every one of the nine historical masks overlaps another of the nine above the
  literal directional 70% / 3-pixel limit; original underlying surfaces were
  unavailable. These files are historical, not novel submissions.

With `G` positive hidden pixels, the organizer defines `T = TP_w`, `F = FP_w`,
and `FN_w = G − T`. Because β=0.8 and α=0.2, the denominator can be written
`D = α(T + F) + βG + ε`, retaining the organizer's stabilizing ε. For an added nonnegative prediction, the change is
`ΔT ≥ 0` and `ΔF ≥ 0`; it improves DTI precisely when
`ΔT·D > αT(ΔT + ΔF)`. Spacing predictions can therefore avoid paying
additional false-positive cost for overlapping credit, while deleting low-yield
points may help **or hurt** depending on their hidden-truth credit. Known-fault
pixels are ignored, but excluding a surrounding buffer is not guaranteed to
help: a genuinely new fault can lie near an existing one.

The score receipt and hidden positives are unavailable. Therefore none of these
mechanistic explanations establishes why the historical number was reported,
how many hidden true/false positives were gained or lost, or whether a new
candidate can beat it. No calibrated mapping from catalogue holdout to public
or private score exists in this checkout. The apparent difference in user
reports is not a controlled causal experiment.

## One-witness impossibility for a champion-sized budget

Using the [pinned lattice](https://github.com/buffedlizard55-lab/13GEMSDOE/blob/92ee6f074323f161eed837b80ec3ee5b662e26f5/docs/downloads/13gems_20261001_r13-lattice-s5_v2_nan-outside.tif)
and the hash-matched **owner mirror** sample as a footprint, Euclidean distance
≤3 px covers 5,160,724 of 5,167,373 cells; 6,649 are uncovered. For `K` distinct
in-footprint dots at most 6,649 can avoid this witness, so the overlap is at
least `max(0,K−6649)/K`. With K=37,654 this is 31,005/37,654 = 82.3418%,
which exceeds 70% regardless of model. The first budget mathematically forced
above 70% is 22,164. These are GEOMETRY-AUDIT quantities, not scores. The
boundary is included to match the prompt's “within 3 px”; the metric's
triangular weight at exactly 300 m is zero. This result only proves a stop for
the named witness and budget. A small-budget candidate can still fail; we did
not search the uncovered cells to game the check.

## Irregularities and next work

The GitHub-only feed detected that GEMSDOE25 changed after the initial review.
At [commit 19b219f](https://github.com/buffedlizard55-lab/GEMSDOE25/blob/19b219f629440b9975b093f459c74e21a42476ca/docs/data/run_card_h79_cthm_2026-10-27.json),
its H79 research combines potential fields, conductivity and other physical
operators; its own card reports a negative verdict and a literal lattice stop,
not a slot approval or organizer score. This is relevant prior art for the
proposed cross-profile idea. We did not reproduce that experiment or exhaustively
re-audit the updated repository, so the feed remains marked **changed since
review** and no broader novelty or current-registry pass is claimed.

1. **No assigned lane.** Four qualitative, distinct proposals are listed in
   [the preregistration](hypotheses-2026-10-27.md); none is an assignment.
2. **Incomplete current registry.** Nine pinned historical files plus a lattice
   are enough to demonstrate failure, never enough to certify uniqueness for a
   new candidate. Acquire an independently complete shared parallel inventory.
3. **Mirror is not an official sample receipt.** Its 60,988 positive sample
   pixels exactly equal its label mask, while the organizer describes a
   total-absence sample. Preserve it as a *provisional* grid only; compare to a
   fresh authenticated organizer file without storing credentials.
4. **Shared-tool continuous-probability defect.** The pinned
   [evaluator](https://github.com/buffedlizard55-lab/GEMSDOE32/blob/73485f27cf230af770167397c2b72cb9ce3b3f25/scripts/evaluate_holdout.py#L38-L56)
   and [writer](https://github.com/buffedlizard55-lab/GEMSDOE32/blob/73485f27cf230af770167397c2b72cb9ce3b3f25/scripts/submission_writer.py#L40-L70)
   convert every positive input to `True`, even though their public interfaces
   accept fields/probabilities. The metric permits continuous probabilities.
   The evaluator's static-file mode also cannot prove the model was rebuilt
   from visible faults in each fold. Do not use the tools as continuous or
   fold-safe by assumption. The upstream owner needs to fix and regression-test
   the shared template once; this checkout has not created a private fork or
   silently claimed the fix. Binary-only runs would still need all other gates.
   These are the GEMSDOE32 `scripts/` CLI entrypoints, not the distinct vendored
   `gems57` evaluator and writer used by H1; the latter preserve float values
   when computing the soft metric or writing a raster. This distinction does
   not independently reproduce or validate H1's recorded holdout.
5. **Portal-range ambiguity.** The independent validator rejects every NaN,
   infinity, masked cell, sentinel or out-of-range value *inside* the footprint.
   It reports both strict NaN-outside and whole-array finite/range diagnostics.
   Requiring NaN outside the footprint is this project's conservative
   interpretation of the published null/NaN wording, not an organizer receipt;
   the exact reason for the user's prior portal error remains unknown.
6. **Proxy and confounders.** Hidden labels may differ spatially and geologically
   from catalogue faults. Pluvial shorelines, lithologic contacts, volcanic
   boundaries, gravity terrain effects, cultural magnetic noise, brine, and
   non-geothermal groundwater can all mimic one or more fault/vent indicators.
   AUC >0.90 on any single fold feature is a leakage alarm, not a discovery.
7. **Future evaluation.** For an assigned lane, use visible faults only in each
   buffered whole-segment fold, perform canaries before fitting, score pooled
   DTI with spatial-block 95% intervals against the frozen same-fold best,
   check the complete registry before and after placement, then validate the
   saved bytes. Promotion and weekly-slot selection remain a separate step.
