3. What may explain GEMSDOE32's reported 0.2778

The plausible mechanism is pruning predictions near already mapped faults, which may reduce false-positive mass while a distance-tolerant kernel still gives partial credit to nearby faults missing from the public catalogue. The official metric uses α=0.2, β=0.8 and a triangular 300 m kernel, so that tradeoff is scientifically plausible. It is not established as the cause of the reported value.

The official DTI has separate truth-side and prediction-side weighted sums. Dot-count arithmetic alone cannot reconstruct hidden truth, true-positive credit, or false-positive mass. The 0.2778 remains USER-REPORTED / UNVERIFIED; the historical source says the contemporaneous file was unscored, and no later organizer receipt tying this hash to 0.2778 was found. Treat the explanation as a falsifiable hypothesis, not a result. The task target is fault structures indicative of geothermal resources—not direct geothermal vents.

See the independent H33 review, the unassigned next-lane slate, and the historical calibration record (the last is exploratory and is not used as a score).
