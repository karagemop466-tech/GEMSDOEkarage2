How to submit (DrivenData form guide)

  1. Log in to DrivenData and enter the GEMS Prize Challenge competition page.
  2. Open "Make a Submission" and attach a single-band float32 GeoTIFF.
  3. Use the official template for CRS, shape, geotransform and exterior/nodata requirements. Validate the full raster and investigate the organizer’s instructions on outside-footprint values before upload.
  4. Only after every scientific and uniqueness gate passes and the separate selector authorizes a slot, upload that approved file with its short note.
  5. Wait for the organizer result; local holdout DTI is not a leaderboard score.
