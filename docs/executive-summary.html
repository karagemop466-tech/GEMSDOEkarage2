Validation standard

What “OK to submit” must mean

  • Blocked hide-and-recover with whole fault segments withheld plus a buffer; pixel-exact masking of visible faults.
  • All catalogue-derived features are built only from visible faults. Test each candidate feature alone; AUC > 0.90 is leakage until disproven.
  • Report pooled DTI with alpha 0.2, beta 0.8, triangular 300 m kernel, evaluator version, withheld-positive count, and 95% CI.
  • Check rank correlation and 3-pixel dot overlap against every registry raster, both before and after final placement.
  • Pass format checks: single-band float32, [0, 1] values, no NaN inside footprint, correct outside NoData, exact CRS/shape/transform/bounds, and a SHA-256.

Submission path

How to submit after all gates pass

  1. Download only the artifact labeled OK TO SUBMIT by the project's validator; this checkout currently has no such artifact.
  2. Open the official competition page and sign in to the authorized account.
  3. Choose New submission, upload the single-band GeoTIFF, and enter the generated unique submission name and short note (140 characters maximum).
  4. Review the competition's displayed submission and score/receipt. Preserve the receipt as the source for any ORGANIZER-CONFIRMED value.

The competition data page may require login. This process cannot authenticate to or bypass the competition data gate.
