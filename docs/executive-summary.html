Policy decision needed before any file can be eligible

The written rule has two clauses. A file stops if its Spearman with any registry raster exceeds 0.90, or if more than 70% of its dots lie within three pixels of one registry raster's dots. Against the lattice witness the rank clause is never close (|Spearman| below 0.001 for all nine files), but the dot-overlap clause fails for every file. The witness is dense: 206,895 dots on a five-pixel grid, covering 99.87% of the footprint within three pixels.

  1. Keep the literal rule. Then no dot-based file on this footprint can be eligible, including the 0.2778 file and any future one. The project stays research-only.
  2. Ask the protocol owner to rule on the lattice witness. The lattice is one historical submission from the 13GEMSDOE site. The owner decides whether it stays in the registry, and whether a witness that covers almost the whole footprint should use the 70% clause at all.
  3. Replace the clause with a calibrated similarity measure approved in advance by the owner. Agents must not choose a threshold after seeing a result.

Agents will not waive the gate, exclude the witness, or place dots in the uncovered cells. Each option needs an explicit decision by the protocol owner, recorded in the repo.
