2 · Why GEMSDOE32's 0.2778 is the family best (byte-verified)

GEMSDOE25 and GEMSDOE30 have identical decoded dot sets ("D2.8"). GEMSDOE31 = D2.8 minus dots ≤1 px from known faults. GEMSDOE32 = D2.8 minus dots ≤2 px (6,436 deleted). GEMSDOE36 = all of GEMSDOE32 + 1,200 dots. Each site's dots are 86.7–100% within 3 px of the champion's. Because organizers exclude known-fault pixels from scoring (staff clarification), apron dots mostly pay false-positive cost (α = 0.2) for credit a neighbour dot already earns under the 300 m max-kernel — deleting them raises DTI mechanically. The champion is an emission improvement on one shared geography, not a new detector. Beating it needs a better surface with the same emission discipline.
