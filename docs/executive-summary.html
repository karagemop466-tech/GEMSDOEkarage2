How to submit (DrivenData form guide)

  1. Log in to DrivenData and enter the GEMS Prize Challenge competition page.
  2. Open "Make a Submission" and attach a single-band float32 GeoTIFF.
  3. Format contract: EPSG:32611, 100 m, bounds [243350, 4135550, 572550, 4508550], 3730×3292 pixels, values in [0,1], NaN outside the footprint.
  4. Only after all gates and the separate selector approve a future candidate, upload it and confirm the [0,1] range. No current artifact is approved.
  5. Wait for the organizer DTI evaluation against organizer labels (α=0.2, β=0.8, R=300 m triangular kernel).

This repo's H-KARAGE-1 TIF is NOT approved to submit: the recorded lattice overlap exceeds the uniqueness limit and no validated holdout improvement or selector approval exists. An owner-mirrored dataset was previously restored, but is not organizer-authenticated.
