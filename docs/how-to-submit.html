Do not submit the research files

There is no approved submission TIFF. The legacy irregular-dot raster fails the lattice duplicate gate and places positive pixels outside the owner-mirrored footprint. Its local range check is not approval. The H1 surface also has a negative verdict. Saved-byte audit · H1 run card.

Steps after a future candidate passes every gate

  1. Check the project’s separate selector decision and current account-specific weekly quota. Do not use a slot for a negative or untested candidate.
  2. Download the exact approved TIFF and compare its SHA-256 with the accompanying run card. Currently no such download exists.
  3. Confirm the file against the authenticated organizer template: one band, matching CRS, shape and transform; finite predictions in [0, 1] inside the footprint. The exterior encoding is unresolved in this repo; a mirror or local preflight cannot prove portal acceptance.
  4. On DrivenData, sign in, open the competition’s submission form, and attach the approved TIFF (or a ZIP containing one TIFF).
  5. Enter the approved run card’s short note (at most 140 characters by this project’s policy) and submit. Record the organizer receipt and exact file hash; an upload or local holdout is not a leaderboard score.

If the form rejects the value range

Reopen the exact uploaded file and inspect its actual saved pixels, masks, nodata, infinities and values. A range error does not by itself establish which of these caused the rejection. The conflict between the published exterior guidance and a reported range error remains unresolved; do not use a competition slot to guess.
