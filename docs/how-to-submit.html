When a future candidate passes all gates

  1. Verify the complete, current registry, assigned lane, independent per-feature leakage canaries, buffered whole-segment holdout and improvement against a compatible frozen incumbent. Honor the immediate duplicate stop; do not place final dots after a surface failure.
  2. Use the shared writer and validate the saved bytes against an organizer-authenticated template: single-band GeoTIFF, matching CRS, shape and transform, finite values in [0,1] inside the footprint. Resolve exterior nodata policy against the organizer instructions; a local check does not prove portal acceptance.
  3. Obtain a separate selector decision and check the account's remaining weekly quota. Do not use a slot just because a file passes format checks.
  4. On the DrivenData competition page, log in and select the approved TIFF (or ZIP containing one TIFF) under “File to submit.” Add its unique name and the accompanying note (at most 140 characters). Confirm the form and preserve the file-bound organizer receipt.

Range error

The reported “Predicted values must be in range [0, 1]” error does not identify the offending pixels or prove its cause. Check the exact saved TIFF for out-of-range values, NaN, infinity and nodata, including exterior cells, and compare it with the official template. Do not assume a format pass guarantees organizer acceptance.
