Why 0.2778 won, in one line of algebra

The organizer metric is DTI = T / (0.2·T + 0.2·F + 0.8·G) with F = n − M. Mapped faults are masked out of the truth, so a dot within the 3 px kernel of the catalogue is a near-certain false positive that costs 0.2 and earns nothing. GEMSDOE32 is the same dot lineage as its siblings with 6,436 apron dots deleted: that alone moved 0.2600 → 0.2778.

Reproduced exactlyn = 37,654, T = 5,073.29, M = 1,038.49, F = 36,615.51, G = 12,405.77 → DTI 0.2778 = the reported value. The metric model is verified, not assumed.
