THE B2 FORENSIC AUDIT

Why might GEMSDOE32 have led the supplied list?

The independent raster audit reconstructs the GEMSDOE32 B2 positive mask by removing 2,545 pixels near the mapped-fault mirror from the GEMSDOE31 solo mask; it adds none. GEMSDOE25 and GEMSDOE30 have the same dot mask. This is a verified relationship among pinned owner files, not an organizer score receipt.

The official metric uses a maximum distance-weighted contribution for each truth cell while positive predictions can add false-positive mass. Reducing redundant predictions could improve the index, but some deleted predictions could have been true positives. Hidden truth prevents a causal conclusion. A new method could exceed the reported value, but none here has demonstrated that.

Scope matters. The site reviews nine historical files and one registry witness, not all simultaneous work. It cannot certify a new surface or a complete-registry pass.
