Why GEMSDOE32 scored 0.2778 and how this file tries to beat it

The nine files share one ridge-dot field. GEMSDOE32's B2 file is that field with every dot within 2 px of a mapped fault deleted: because the scorer excludes mapped-fault pixels and almost no hidden fault runs that close to a mapped one, those ≈ 6,400 dots were pure false-positive mass. Removing them raised the family from 0.2600 to 0.2778 without finding a single new fault. The score identity DTI = T / (0.2·T + 0.2·F + 0.8·G), pinned by the r13 lattice (0.0904), puts the hidden truth at G ≈ 12,406 pixels and B2 at T ≈ 5,073 credit (≈ 41 % weighted coverage), 1,038 hit-dots, 36,616 false dots.

HK3 keeps the geometry lessons (3 px catalogue apron, 3 px spacing, binary dots) and changes the surface: eight catalogue-free edge-geometry bands (gradient-ridge centrality and structure-tensor coherence of detrended elevation, gravity and magnetic tilt-angle zero-crossing distances, 500 m roughness) added to the 26-band K2 stack and learned per spatial fold from visible faults only. On identical folds it covers 11.4% more withheld fault credit than the D2.8 family field at the same dot count, with 15.6% higher per-dot hit rate. The budget (45,108 dots) was chosen by a pre-registered maximin rule on the calibrated curve, not tuned on the holdout. Details, hypotheses and limits →
