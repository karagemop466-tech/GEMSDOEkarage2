WHY WE STOPPED · GEOMETRY AUDIT, NOT A SCORE

The uniqueness rule has a real, measurable conflict.

The pinned 13GEMSDOE lattice leaves only 6,649 footprint pixels farther than 3 pixels away. For 37,654 distinct dots, at least 82.34% must be within 3 pixels—over the 70% limit, no matter where they are placed. This bound does not establish uniqueness at smaller budgets.

INTERPRETING THE LEADER · 03 / FORENSICS

A lineage, not a causal proof.

The historical GEMSDOE32 raster is the GEMSDOE31 reference with 2,545 dots near mirrored known faults removed; it is not an independent new detector. Sparse, well-spaced predictions can reduce redundant false-positive mass under the distance-weighted metric. But hidden truth and a file-linked score receipt are unavailable, so we cannot prove why the reported value was higher or claim a future result.
