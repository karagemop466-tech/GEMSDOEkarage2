📑 Top candidate: H-KARAGE-2 — why it is contrarian but sound

Everyone in the focus nine maps fault traces (dots on or near mapped structures). The private test set is expert-identified faults not in the public USGS database — i.e., concealed/blind faults. H-KARAGE-2 targets the fluid expression of those faults (radon microseepage via radiometric disequilibrium, spring lines via 10 m topography) rather than the trace itself, so it can score on faults missing from the catalogue.

  • Free official data: USGS 3DEP 1 m/10 m DEM (The National Map, public); GeoDAWN radiometrics (USGS / GDR submission 1391, public). Both named in the competition page as allowed external data.
  • Named non-fault mimic: dry playa/lake shorelines (Lake Lahontan benches), alluvial-fan terrace risers, and lithologic contacts without displacement can produce similar radiometric/topographic patterns — must be deconfounded with gravity/magnetic steps and along-strike continuity.
  • How it differs from the repo: A33-THKCOND uses Th/K ratio × conductivity × gravity margin; H-KARAGE-2 uses eTh disequilibrium (radon proxy) × 10 m spring-convergence topology, neither of which appears in any focus-site submission.
