QUALITATIVE RANK 1 · NOT A SCORE PROJECTION

H1 · Signed downthrow parity

Layers: 12 detrended elevation · 15 basement depth · 17 conductivity

Signature and missing-fault rationale: Opposing signed gradients across 200/600 m scales; masked surface traces are not predictors. Could detect a buried displacement without a visible scarp.

Non-fault mimic: Basin sedimentation, salinity, interpolation seams.

Cost / status: Low · tested; negative, stopped

QUALITATIVE RANK 2 · NOT A SCORE PROJECTION

H2 · Shear-offset edge dipoles

Layers: 2 reduced-to-pole magnetics · 13 isostatic gravity

Signature and missing-fault rationale: Cross-strike edge-offset parity instead of coincident edge strength; buried juxtaposition need not appear in surface mapping.

Non-fault mimic: Intrusive or lithologic contacts.

Cost / status: Medium · deferred, untested

QUALITATIVE RANK 3 · NOT A SCORE PROJECTION

H3 · Conductive hinge / basement inflection

Layers: 15 basement depth · 17 conductivity

Signature and missing-fault rationale: Conductivity ridge transverse to a signed depth-curvature zero crossing; possible buried flexure.

Non-fault mimic: Aquifer boundaries and interpolation seams.

Cost / status: Medium · deferred, untested

QUALITATIVE RANK 4 · NOT A SCORE PROJECTION

H4 · Gravity edge without terrain expression

Layers: 13 isostatic gravity · 12 detrended elevation · 19 elevation slope

Signature and missing-fault rationale: Persistent gravity gradient with weak terrain expression; anti-evidence from scarps rather than the H1 topographic corroboration.

Non-fault mimic: Sediment thickness changes and lithologic contacts.

Cost / status: Low · deferred, untested
